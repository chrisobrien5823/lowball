      How to play
      
      
      Five questions a day, the same for everyone. Each one sets a line.
      
      
        
        
      • MAX questions: your answer has to fit under the line.
        • 
        
      • MIN questions: your answer has to reach the line.
        • 
        
      • The chart draws the value you name, on a real scale. On a MAX question the pen climbs toward the line from below; on a MIN question the chart is flipped and the pen comes down toward the line from above. Crossing the line is always a bust.
        • 
        
      • The closer to the line, the more points, up to 100 per question. The climb is steepest near the line: within 10% of it is worth about 73 points, within 1% about 97. Cross to the wrong side and you bust.
        • 
        
      • "Country" means one of the 193 UN member states. If you type a country outside the question's group, a territory, a country with no data for that question, or something we don't recognize, we'll say why and you can try again. The clock keeps running.
        • 
        
      • Every figure on a question comes from the one source named under its prompt, so you only ever need to trust one.
        • 
        
      • Sound cues mark the pen climbing, a bust, your result, and the last five seconds. Everything you hear is also on screen, and the Sound button in the top bar turns it off for good.
        • 
        
      • A new puzzle opens every day at midnight US Eastern.
